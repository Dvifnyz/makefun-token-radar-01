How the implied value is worked out

For every pre-token protocol, from the snapshot’s figures alone:

implied = TVL × 0.30 × growth × maturity × breadth × category

  • Growth is the seven-day change, clamped to −50% … +100%, weighted 0.6: 1 + growth ÷ 100 × 0.6. No change reported counts as 0%.
  • Maturity is 0.70 + age ÷ 1460 × 0.50, where age is the days since the protocol was first listed with no token; a protocol with no listing date counts as 240 days.
  • Breadth is 1 + chains × 0.03, up to five chains: a protocol live on more chains reaches more holders.
  • Category weighs fee-bearing activity: Dexs 1.15, Lending 1.12, Yield and Yield Aggregator 1.10, Derivatives 1.08, CDP and RWA 1.06, Onchain Capital Allocator and Risk Curators 1.05, every other category 1.00.

The board never says a protocol has no token: the snapshot is DefiLlama’s record, not proof, so each row says the protocol records no token. The implied value is this radar’s own claim, never DefiLlama’s and never a promise that a token will be issued, or what it will be worth if one is.
